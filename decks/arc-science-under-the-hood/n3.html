Snöggo

PublishedComputedStatus: development handoff. Release acceptance is false. The package has produced no result.

Snöggo computes how a change in a molecule changes its binding. We take a real structure, change one thing step by step in the computer, and measure the energy along the way. We do it in the protein with the molecule bound and in plain water. The difference tells us whether the change helps. A team uses this before it pays for a synthesis.

Under the hood

  • Cycle closureup to 1 kcal/molThe two paths around the cycle must agree. A gap means a leg is wrong.
  • Drift between halvesup to 1 kcal/molThe first half of a run and the second half must agree. A gap means it has not settled.
  • Replica spreadup to 1 kcal/molRepeated runs must agree. A gap means luck decided the result.
  • Overlapat least 0.03The forward and reverse changes must overlap. Without overlap they cannot be combined.
  • Work samplesat least 20We count only independent samples. Fewer give a noisy estimate.
