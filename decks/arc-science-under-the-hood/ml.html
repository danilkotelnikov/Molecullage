Learn a velocity along a straight path from noise to data. Lipman and colleagues, 2022.

Symmetry

Moving or rotating a protein does not change it. The model must treat every pose alike. Build that in, or rotate the training examples at random.

Attention

Each residue can look at every other residue, near or far in the chain. Vaswani and colleagues, 2017.

Loss

Loss = coordinate error + residue type error

Both are averaged over real rows only. The mask hides the padding rows from the loss.
