Redesign of Figure 2 of Taylor and Starr 2026, PLoS Pathogens, doi 10.1371/journal.ppat.1014074. Licence CC BY 4.0 (read at Europe PMC and in the article). The original is not reproduced here; open it at the DOI.

**The original** stacks two heat maps of 200 sites (panel A, KP.3.1.1 over LP.8.1). The ACE2-contact squares sit only under the lower map, ticks are every fifth site, amino-acid letters are tiny, and missing values are bright yellow. Expression is in panel C, two panels away; panel B has four shaded surfaces with labels on long leaders.

**The redesign** puts the 20 ACE2-contact sites first in wide columns, then the other 180, with both backgrounds on the same columns, so a site reads straight down. Strips give the site mean of binding and of expression. Colour is diverging, white at the unmutated value: purple weaker, green tighter; grey with a diagonal is no value; a dot is the site's own residue. The structure (8QSQ, ACE2 pale grey) is coloured by the binding site mean; an enlargement outlines the paper's hotspot sites 455, 456, 493.

**Numbers.** Every cell is a `delta_bind` of S1 Data (`ppat.1014074.s001.csv`, 677,005 bytes). Site means and contact sites (heavy atoms under 5 Å, the paper's rule, in 8QSQ, 696,028 bytes) are computed here. Spike numbers throughout; 8QSQ chain A is 3 lower (4 lower from 484).

## Checks and open points (for reviewers)

**Ten-second reading.** Almost every mutation weakens ACE2 binding (purple); some columns are pale (tolerant) and the contact block is somewhat darker than the rest, with green cells where E493 changes to D or N. The three outlined sites named in the paper are all ACE2-contact sites.

**What is drawn.** 200 sites (N331 to T531 of the spike without 483, which BA.2.86 and its descendants lack), 4,000 mutations per background (3,800 substitutions and 200 deletions: 19 substitutions and the deletion at each site), 4,200 cells per map with the 200 unmutated residues marked by dots. No binding value in the table: 7 cells (KP.3.1.1) and 17 (LP.8.1), drawn grey with a diagonal and never filled in (`tables/drawn_cells.csv`); no expression value: 6 and 17, which only thin out the expression strips. All other cells are drawn, among them 11 LP.8.1 cells averaged from fewer than 3 barcoded variants (smallest 1; KP.3.1.1 smallest 4). The paper states a minimum of 3 only for the epistatic-shift calculation, not for Fig. 2.

**Scale and signs.** The Methods give binding as -log10(KD), higher = tighter; the S1 Data description says "log10KD" and the paper's Fig. 2A colour bar says "log10 KD" with higher affinity up. The table's values are positive (5.0 to 10.0), so they are -log10(KD) and the sign drawn here is the Methods'. Table ranges of the deltas: binding -4.41 to +0.73, expression -2.09 to +0.51. Scales: binding -4.5 to +1.0, expression -2.5 to +1.0, each side of 0 with its own length, nothing clipped. Same two hues, own scale for each strip; the bar titles say so. The paper gives expression as logMFI; no log base is stated on the figure.

**Site mean (not in the paper).** Plain mean of the measured values of a site's 20 mutations (19 substitutions and the deletion; the unmutated row is excluded), per background and measurement (`tables/site_means.csv`). The paper's Fig. 2B shows an average and a maximum per site; whether its average includes deletions is not stated. Ranges, binding: -3.93 to +0.21 (KP.3.1.1), -4.29 to +0.16 (LP.8.1). Mean of the 20 contact-site means -2.14 against -1.61 for the 180 others (KP.3.1.1; -2.19 and -1.70 for LP.8.1); computed here, not printed on the figure.

**Contact sites.** The paper's legend: RBD residues with non-hydrogen atoms under 5 Å from ACE2 in PDB 8QSQ. Computed here from the coordinates (`src/contacts.py`): 417, 449, 453, 455, 456, 473, 475, 476, 477, 486, 487, 489, 493, 496, 498, 500, 501, 502, 503, 505 (20 sites; `tables/contacts_8QSQ.csv`). The sheet asked for 4.5 Å; that gives 15 sites (without 417, 473, 486, 496, 503) and would drop 417 and 486, two of the sites the paper names, so the paper's 5 Å is used. One line (`sites_50` in `src/scan_data.py`) switches to 4.5 Å. 8QSQ is a 3.7 Å cryo-EM entry (EMD-18639, deposited 2023-10-11), so distances near the cut-off are not sharp.

**Structure and numbering.** 8QSQ is BA.2.86: chain A is the RBD (Spike protein S2'), chain B ACE2 (read from the mmCIF). Its author numbers run 329 to 523 and are not spike numbers: aligning the residue letters to the table's wild-type letters gives spike = author + 3 up to 482 and + 4 from 484 (the entry numbers consecutively after the deleted 483); the script asserts that only documented substitutions differ (455, 456, 493 for both backgrounds; also 346 and 445 for LP.8.1, as in the paper's Fig. 1A). The structure has L455, F456, Q493; KP.3.1.1 and LP.8.1 have S, L, E (read from the table). Table sites without coordinates in 8QSQ: 331, 528, 529, 530, 531 (counted as other sites). The render shows the BA.2.86 structure coloured by data measured in KP.3.1.1 and LP.8.1; it is not a structure of those variants.

**Hotspot sites.** "Sites 455, 456, and 493" (abstract and Results) are the only sites drawn as hotspots. The legend of Fig. 2B labels other sites too (C480, C488, R498, Y501, G502, P486, E493, H445, K478, N487, A475, N417, H505); these are not outlined. Both enlargements, outlines and numbers come from flat colour maps of the render itself (`src/anchors.py`): each number is placed on the most interior pixel of its residue's visible patch and the build checks that the whole number lies inside the outline.

**Where the data and the paper differ.** The Results say residues 475, 478 and 487 "can tolerate many amino acid changes". The table agrees for 478 only: site means for 475, 478, 487 are -2.14, -0.32, -2.87 (KP.3.1.1) and -2.02, -0.29, -2.78 (LP.8.1); every mutation at 475 and 487 lowers binding by 0.5 or more. The data are drawn. The paper's other statements checked against the table: cysteines 480 and 488 (site means -3.91, -3.90) and G502, R498, Y501 (-3.74, -3.60, -3.32) are not tolerated; E493D and E493N raise binding (+0.73, +0.69 in KP.3.1.1). The library counts in Methods (all but 2 and all but 11 of 4,000 mutations sampled) are not comparable with the table's 7 and 17 empty binding cells, which are after filtering.

**Reading choices.** Row order R K H D E Q N S T Y W F A I L M V G P C is the order I read in the paper's Fig. 5B, then a gap (the white line) and the deletion; it is a layout choice, and the figure says so. Contact columns are 2.2 mm wide and the others 0.55 mm; each block sits in a frame with 0.5 mm of padding, so no dot touches the frame. Strips and maps share the columns; the 20 contact labels carry the unmutated letter (same in both backgrounds). The other block has a tick and a label at every tenth site except 500, whose column is in the contact block; labels that would touch are put in a second row. Because the contact sites were taken out, the ticks are unevenly spaced: a grey mark under the map shows each place where one or more were removed, and a triangle (top and bottom of the block) shows where 483 would be. View of the render: line of sight perpendicular to the contact direction at azimuth 30 degrees, picture turned 25 degrees; of the 12 azimuths tried (steps of 30 degrees) it gives the largest visible patch for the smallest of the three hotspot sites; the enlargement is 5 times. ACE2 is the surface of residues with an atom within 5 Å of the RBD (the radius of the contact rule), drawn as a separate pale layer under the RBD, so the picture does not show which parts lie in front. The expression heat maps (panel C of the paper) are not redrawn: only their site means are shown, because two more full maps would add about 90 mm; the cell values are in S1 Data. The sentence on the differences between 8QSQ and the scanned variants (L455S, F456L, Q493E for both; R346T, H445R for LP.8.1 only) is generated by `src/make_plots.py` from every site where a background's wild type differs from the 8QSQ residue, and so is the list of sites without coordinates.

**Original figure.** Read from the figure images in Europe PMC's `supplementaryFiles` archive for PMC13309016 (kept in the scratchpad, not in the repository). The legend and text came from the Europe PMC full-text XML. S1 Data came from the PLOS server (HTTP 200); the same bytes are in the Europe PMC archive (the `bin/` address returned HTTP 403). `fetch_data.py` pins both files by SHA-256 (`data/SOURCES.txt`, git-ignored).

**Build checks** (`checkAlign`, 0.25 mm unless noted, measured on the rendered page): frames, strips and maps on their data; first and last column one padding inside the frame; hotspot boxes on their columns; site labels centred on their columns and ticks; amino-acid letters on their rows and at least 1 mm from the frame; bar labels on their ticks and no caption longer than its bar; dashed box inside the overview and with the aspect of the enlargement; outlines inside the enlargement; numbers inside their outlines; no text on text; nothing within 1 mm of the page edge.

**QA** (`qa_fig.py`, normal): ink 36 %, empty cells 1.3 %, longest empty band 1.6 %, largest blank 1.8 %, smallest type 6.55 pt, one page, Arial only, 178 mm wide (215.8 mm tall; the toolkit's 170 mm plot helper is not used, the layer is drawn on a canvas of its own). Words 397 against 130 (warning): 116 are amino-acid letters, site numbers and tick labels of a 2 x 4,200 cell data figure; the rest are titles, definitions, key entries and notes.

**After the accuracy and blind reads.** Added: the plain sentence on what a column, a row, purple, green and white mean; `del` and the white line; the hatched cell and the counts of cells without value (binding and expression, per background); the row order; the rule for the 20 and 180 split in the block headings; the strips named `site mean, binding` and `site mean, expression`, each scale named after the strips it colours; the key line of symbols (the hotspot key now stands alone); site 483 marked; every tenth site labelled; a colour bar, the grey radius, the meaning of white, the sites missing from 8QSQ and the BA.2.86 statement in the head of panel b; the complete list of 8QSQ differences. Letters sit 1.5 mm from the frame and the row pitch grew from 1.95 to 2.1 mm. Accuracy NITs applied: `heavy atom`, the strip scales, `(paper's Fig. 1A)` for 483. Not changed on purpose: the title keeps `every RBD mutation` (the scan's aim; the head names the cells without value), the 11 LP.8.1 cells from fewer than 3 barcodes are listed in `tables/drawn_cells.csv`, and the 475/478/487 wording stays in this file only.
