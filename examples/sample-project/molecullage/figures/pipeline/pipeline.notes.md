# pipeline

**Message.** Sequences are folded, docked to the antigen, then scored and ranked into a list of best candidates.

**Template.** templates/pipeline.html of Molecullage, cut to two rows: three stage strips (coloured bars that name the stages), three tool blocks with numbered badges, pale pills (rounded labels) for the data, a key.

**Data.** None. The figure shows steps, not results. The sizes in the pills are placeholders (d numbers per item) and the footnote says so. The step names come from the caption of the original Figure 2.

**Claim boundary.** Sizes are placeholders: d numbers per item.

**QA.** qa_figure, strictness normal: ink 74.5 %, cells 9.6 %, band 2.8 %, blank 2.5 %, type 6.55 pt, Arial only, width 178.1 mm, 56 words. All checks pass. The pale column panels (palesky token) carry the ink and keep the empty-space checks low.

**Alignment.** Every block is centred on the axis of its column; the page script throws if one is not, and if the two hand-over arrows do not lie on the row centre.

**Reads.** Blind read, round 1 (2026-10-17, fresh agent, PNG only). Found: the key said "structures" for a pink box named "Poses". Fixed: the key says "models and poses". Not changed: the numbered badges overlap the block corners by design. Round 2 not run yet.

**Ten-second sentence.** Sequences go through folding, docking and scoring to a ranked list.
