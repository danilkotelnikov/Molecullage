# How the structures example was made

- Page: `templates/structures.html`. The renders come from `scripts/render_examples.py`: ChimeraX 1.10, flat lighting, silhouettes, 2400 x 1800 transparent PNG, one crop per render.
- Panel a: PDB 1MEL (deposited 1996-06-06), chain A (single-domain antibody) and chain L (lysozyme).
- Panel b: PDB 7Z0X (deposited 2022-02-23), chains H and L (Fab) and R (spike S1 domain).
- Panel c: PDB 1IGT (deposited 1996-10-25), chains A, B, C and D (whole IgG).
- All entries are public and were checked against data.rcsb.org on 2026-10-16.
