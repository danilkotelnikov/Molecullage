# Blind-read brief

Give an independent reviewer the PNG and nothing else: no notes, no caption, no page source, no other figure.

> You see one figure from a paper in structural biology. You have no other text.
> 1. In two sentences, what does it show? Say what object, what moves or changes, and what the point is.
> 2. What could you not interpret? List each label, symbol, colour, arrow or panel that you could not explain, and why.
> 3. What looks wrong? Look for legends that do not match the marks, shapes that suggest the wrong number, a pointer that ends on the wrong part, a list written as a range, text that overlaps or is clipped, an arrow that misses, and a mark that sits at an outlier.
> Answer in that order. Quote the figure's own words in quotation marks. Do not guess what the authors meant: say "unclear" instead.

How to use the answer: fix every item of questions 2 and 3, rebuild, run the read again with a fresh reviewer or the same one, and write the final two-sentence answer to question 1 in the notes file as the ten-second sentence. If it differs from the message you intended, the figure is not finished.

The reviewer is "an independent reviewer": any capable reader that has not seen the notes.
